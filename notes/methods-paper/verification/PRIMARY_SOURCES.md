# Primary sources and normalization provenance

Checked against primary sources on 27 November 2026. The bibliography in
`references.bib` records the versions used. These references support the
specified background inputs; they do not substitute for the manuscript's
proofs or its exact computational checks.

| Source | Use in this manuscript |
|---|---|
| [Li, Liu and Chang, version 2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.24160v2) | Original holomorphic current-area construction and displacement convention. The repository's supplied TeX source is `sources/LLC_main.tex`, especially `eq-operatope`. The minus sign for each chord and the regrouping of the current-area form give the explicit conversion in Section 2 of the methods manuscript. The repository scalar convention is stated separately. |
| [Wang, version 4](https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.08113v4) | Holomorphic graph-integral compactification and finiteness setting. The present graph-specific convergence argument is also given explicitly. |
| [Gui, Wang and Williams, version 2](https://arxiv.org/html/2510.26608v2) | Theorem 4.13 already supplies an all-order cube recursion for Type I' Laman weights. Remark 4.14 discusses the relation to Schwinger parameters. The methods manuscript credits this all-order result and does not reinterpret its examples through three loops as a limitation of the theorem. |
| [Gaiotto, Kulp and Wu](https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.13049) | Higher-operation context; the manuscript does not claim to originate the corresponding identities. |
| [Grothendieck](https://www.numdam.org/item/PMIHES_1966__29__95_0/) | Algebraic de Rham comparison and finite-dimensional cohomology, applied to the smooth affine sector opens and their face intersections. |
| [Bloch, Esnault and Kreimer](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0510011) | Relative-cohomological interpretation of graph periods. |
| [Brown](https://arxiv.org/abs/1512.06409) | Graph periods and polynomial numerators; in particular Section 6.7. |
| [Panzer](https://ems.press/journals/aihpd/articles/8830429) | Greedy Hepp-sector description and positive power counting, Lemma 2.8 and Proposition 2.9. |
| [Oaku and Takayama](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/9801114) | Algorithmic algebraic de Rham calculations. The cited journal article is from 1999; the internal BibTeX key is not a publication date. |
| [Walther](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/9807176) | Algorithmic de Rham cohomology in the discussion of a possible complete computational procedure. |
| [Smirnov and Petukhov](https://arxiv.org/abs/1004.4199) | General finiteness of master-integral families. This is background, not a proof of the particular numerical basis or threshold of an arbitrary graph. |
