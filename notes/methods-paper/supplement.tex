(01,13,02,24,05,06,35,36,45,46,56).
\]
The independent source spinors are $\lambda_1,\lambda_2,\lambda_5$,
with $\lambda_6=-\lambda_1-\lambda_2-\lambda_5$ and
$\lambda_0=\lambda_3=\lambda_4=0$. The marked vertices are $5,6$.
Every bracket is the determinant of its two displayed source spinors.
In each row multiply the complete displayed monomial by the rational
column plus the second column times $\zeta(3)$ plus the third column
times $\zeta(5)$. Sum every row in the indicated original edge.
All unlisted monomial coefficients are exactly zero. The free spinor
index $+\dot a$ is retained in every current row, and every bracket
power is nonnegative. The result therefore applies at degenerate source
frames as well.

These are normalized geometric kernels in two holomorphic directions
and no topological direction, before Wick, colour and coupling
assembly. The first-current insertion uses the negative displacement
derivative convention of the main manuscript. There are sixteen
nonzero bare monomials and 468 nonzero current monomials, carrying
1,334 nonzero rational current coefficients. All source data come from
the complete exact certificate in
\path{explore/circuit-general/forest-source-currents/certificate.json}
at repository commit \texttt{75a9218}. The generator and an independent
table parser check every printed monomial and rational coefficient.
