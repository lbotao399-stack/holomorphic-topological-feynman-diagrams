\section{Consequences and remaining questions}
\label{discussion:scope}

The framework gives a sequence of exact reductions with different
outputs. Local source elimination produces the full current map.
Compact cubes give an exact convergent representation of the
integral at every finite current order. Relative cohomology proves
that, for a fixed normalized scalar core, all positive integer
pole orders occupy one finite rational span. A successful finite
relative reduction supplies an explicit target identity, while
the wheel construction identifies its complete stable scalar
span uniformly.

These results explain why repeated derivative and dimension
calculations on a fixed core can reuse period data. A displacement
derivative inserts an original current; after scalar reduction it
changes polynomial numerator powers and integer denominator
powers. It need not enlarge the universal span associated to the
fixed relative algebraic pair. It can nonetheless access a
direction missing from the bare kernel or from a restricted
source assignment. The nonzero current with zero bare value in
Section~\ref{currents:family} is a concrete example.

\subsection{A period-valued product can obey a rational differential}

The quantum product and its differential satisfy identities of
the complete higher operations \cite{gaiotto2024,li2026}.
Such identities constrain the assembled graph sum; they do not
require each individual geometric graph kernel to be rational.
This can already be seen from elementary linear algebra. Let a
linear map $D$ be defined over $\Q$ on a finite coefficient space,
and suppose a required correction satisfies $D m=b$ with rational
$b$. If this rational linear system is consistent after extending
the coefficient field, rational row reduction gives a rational
particular solution $m_0$. Every solution is
\begin{equation}
 m=m_0+z,\qquad D z=0.
 \label{discussion:homogeneous}
\end{equation}
The closed correction $z$ may have period-valued coefficients.
It need not be exact under any preceding differential. At later
orders, period-valued lower-order products can also enter the
forcing term $b$. Therefore rational supercharge kernels alone
do not prove cancellation of every zeta value in a general
higher-superpotential product. A cancellation statement requires
the complete Wick/colour/representation sum or an additional
identity for its period-valued homogeneous directions.

Equation~\eqref{discussion:homogeneous} is a statement about a
rational linear system, not an assumption of arithmetic
independence of zeta values. If several periods satisfy rational
relations, take their actual finite rational span before choosing
coefficient coordinates.

\subsection{Open computational problems}

For a general irreducible core, Theorem~\ref{periods:finite-span}
does not identify the numerical constants or the first stable
pole order. In particular the finite tables for the triangular
prism, $K_{3,3}$ and $K_5$ do not establish all-integer-power
closure in $\Q+\Q\zeta(3)+\Q\zeta(5)$. The source family whose
scalar core is $K_{3,3}$ with an additional edge still needs its
denominator-power-eight current reduction. These questions remain
open after the complete physical-quartic calculation.

The compact atlas and exact relative search can both become
large. The rational series has a rigorous finite sufficient order
for any positive requested rational error, but the theorem gives
no practical polynomial complexity bound. A general de Rham
basis computation with explicit reduction witnesses would be a
stronger algorithmic result than a bounded successful target
search.

The purely holomorphic scalar theorem also does not account for
topological contact distributions in a general $T=1$ theory.
Finally, all displayed forest results are geometric kernels.
Constructing a physical operator product requires the complete
input derivatives, graph symmetries, Wick signs, representation
tensors and coupling factors. The retained full-current formulas
provide the required geometric data without asserting that
this physical assembly has been carried out for every theory.
