\begin{abstract}
We present an exact computational framework for holomorphic graph kernels
with arbitrary retained sources and finite current insertions. Local
network elimination keeps the affine source flow and every original
current. A compact spanning-tree atlas converts the complete current-area
form into rational integrals on closed cubes and gives a convergent
rational series with an explicit finite formula for its remainder.
For a fixed Kirchhoff core, we prove that all absolutely convergent
normalized periods with rational polynomial numerators and positive
integer denominator powers lie in a finite rational vector space.
The full scalar spaces are nested and eventually stabilize. Their
relative boundary relations yield complete, independently verifiable
target-reduction certificates. For an $n$-spoke wheel the full scalar span
is $\Q+\sum_{j=2}^{n-1}\Q\zeta(2j-1)$ at every integer denominator
power $q\ge\max(3,n-1)$. A five-loop quartic product forest illustrates
the method: all eleven first-current spinors are obtained as complete
source polynomials, including degenerate source frames. The accompanying
supplement prints all 468 nonzero current monomials and their 1,334
nonzero rational period coefficients. Exact certificates, replay scripts,
normalizations and the remaining physical assembly questions are stated
explicitly.
\end{abstract}
