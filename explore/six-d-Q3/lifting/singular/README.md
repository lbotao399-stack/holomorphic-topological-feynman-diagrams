# Restriction to the regular Cartan at finite source weight

For relative local operators of $\mathfrak{sl}_2$, assign to a divided Taylor coefficient the three holomorphic exponents and its two source odd-coordinate weights. Put $D$ equal to their sum. At every tested multidegree the exact calculation compares three spaces: classical relative cohomology, its restriction to jets of a single Cartan, and the subspace generated by products of strict quadratic classes.

## Matrices

Use $[H,E]=2E$, $[H,F]=-2F$, $[E,F]=H$. The relative variables are the $E,H,F$ components of each Taylor coefficient of $x=g_1,y=g_2,b,c$. Omit $c(0)$; the odd generators are $b,c$. The differential is the indicated coefficient of
$$
\widehat Q_0 c=c^2,\qquad
\widehat Q_0 x=[c,x],\qquad
\widehat Q_0 y=[c,y],\qquad
\widehat Q_0 b=[c,b]+[x,y].
$$
For divided Taylor coefficients the product sums carry no binomial prefactors. At fixed weight and field length form all supercommutative monomials. In Cartan charge zero impose invariance by the raising operator on coordinate polynomials, given on a generic matrix $X$ by $\delta X=[F,X]$. On components it takes $X_E$ to zero, $X_H$ to $-X_E$ and $X_F$ to $2X_H$. Its charge-zero kernel consists exactly of the trivial representations. Grouping source monomials by mode multiset makes this kernel calculation independent for each multiset. A spin-one singlet tensor depends only on the multiplicities and exterior powers of equal modes, so the computation reuses it for every matching mode multiset. Each singlet obtained by nullspace elimination has a private free column; retaining those coefficients of each differential image gives all its singlet coordinates. The exhaustive matrix calculation uses primitive integer rows and rationally invertible echelon steps. At $D\le6$ a second calculation recomputes cohomology on the full Cartan charges zero and one and subtracts their dimensions field length by field length. [Matrix implementation](relative.py)

On the resulting singlet space of length $L$ let $Q_L$ denote the full differential matrix, and let $R_L$ set all off-diagonal component variables to zero. The latter is defined on all jets of $x,y,b$ and all positive jets of $c$. The Cartan differential is zero. Thus
$$
\begin{aligned}
\dim H^L&=\dim C^L-\operatorname{rank}Q_L-\operatorname{rank}Q_{L-1},\\
\operatorname{rank}\big(R_L\vert_{H^L}\big)
 &=\operatorname{rank}\binom{Q_L}{R_L}-\operatorname{rank}Q_L .
\end{aligned}
$$
Every matrix square is checked on the singlet basis. The alternating singlet count is also recomputed from the difference of adjacent Cartan-weight coefficients of the exterior and symmetric power series. [Independent index](index_scan.py)

For each two-mode pairing form its defining trace. Take the kernel of the full classical differential on these invariant bilinears and restrict to the diagonal. Every resulting cohomology class has a strict quadratic representative. Starting from the unit, multiply the restricted polynomials by these generators and reduce to an independent basis at each multidegree, retaining all odd factors with Koszul signs. These are the diagonal values of the *classical leading terms* of finite marked products. Equality of this dimension with $\dim H$ and with the restriction rank proves both spanning and independence of the global leading classes. [Product calculation](diagonal_products.py) · [Strict quadratics](../quadratic.md)

## Quantum conclusion

Every class so spanned has a finite marked lift. Injectivity of its restriction to diagonal jets implies injectivity on the regular locus, since nonzero diagonal polynomials remain nonzero after inverting the discriminant. No incoming differential can kill one of these classes: at page one the restriction is injective, the localized spectral sequence collapses, and induction passes the injection to each subsequent page on this multidegree. The absolute constant-ghost factor is restored by the marked chain splitting. [Regular meromorphic complex](../one_jet/regular.md) · [Absolute splitting](../relative.md)

Through $D=8$, with at least two holomorphic derivatives, the full computation covers 188 orbits under permutations of the three holomorphic directions and exchange of the two odd coordinates. These give 1,134 labeled multidegrees and 20,407 relative classes. Every class survives and the singular restriction kernel is zero. The exhaustive run checks every field length and includes weight slices with empty cohomology. [Exhaustive replay](finite.py)

A separate index screen in the rectangular domain $0\le\alpha_i\le2$, $0\le p,q\le3$ reaches $D=12$. The Euler characteristic equals the quadratic-product dimension at all 432 weights, with 27,706 independent product classes across 422 nonempty sectors. Two opposite-parity singular classes could cancel in this index screen. The full relative differential ranks above provide the stronger conclusion in their stated range.


## Balanced weights and certified rational ranks

Fix the total holomorphic degree $h$ and the total odd source weight $t$. The kernel of restriction and the quotient of relative cohomology by the span of quadratic products are polynomial $GL_3\times GL_2$ modules at these fixed degrees, using pullback of source coordinates. If a polynomial irreducible $GL_k$ module of degree $d$ occurs, it has a weight whose entries differ by at most one. Indeed from any weight vector having two entries differing by at least two, the lowering operator of the corresponding root acts nontrivially: it is injective on positive-weight spaces in each finite-dimensional $\mathfrak{sl}_2$ string. Repeating this decreases the sum of squares of the entries until they differ by at most one. Permutation matrices order those entries as desired. Thus it suffices to prove vanishing for the separately balanced weight of the $h$ and $t$ directions. This argument applies separately at each field length. [Exact balanced computation](balanced.py)

For larger matrices, reduce the integer differential on singlets modulo the prime 127, inverting the nonzero coefficients of the private coordinate columns. A nonzero minor modulo 127 is nonzero over the rationals. Thus the two computed differential ranks give an upper bound for rational cohomology. Independently, the rational dimension of the Cartan restrictions of quadratic products bounds this cohomology from below. Equality gives an exact rational dimension and proves injectivity and spanning, even if one has only used modular elimination for the upper bound. One Python integer packs seven-bit residues in fifteen-bit lanes. In a pivot operation the unreduced lane is at most $126^2+126<2^{14}$; folding the upper seven bits uses $2^7=1$ in the field. No carry reaches an adjacent lane. [Packed rank certificate](modular.py)

At $D=9$ the separately balanced weights with $h=2,3,4,5,6,7,8,9$ have relative dimensions $19,84,176,314,361,304,118,44$. In each case the quadratic-product lower bound attains the rational dimension. The polynomial-module argument carries this to all multidegrees at $D=9$. Summing the adjacent Cartan-weight index, including the earlier degrees, gives 60,527 relative classes with $h\ge2$ through $D=9$. Adding the two previously solved sectors $h=0,1$ gives 60,905, all permanent. [Zero derivatives](../sl2_zero_jets.md) · [One derivative](../one_jet/README.md)

## Sparse Krylov certificate

Let $A$ be the sparse singlet differential over the prime field of order $65521$. With invertible diagonal matrices $D_1,D_2$, use $M=D_2A^{\mathsf T}D_1A$, transposing $A$ first when this reduces the size of $M$. For arbitrary vectors $u,v$, every term of the scalar sequence $u^{\mathsf T}M^{j+1}v$, $j\ge0$, uses a vector in $\operatorname{im}M$. Cayley–Hamilton on this space gives a recurrence of degree at most $\operatorname{rank}M\le\operatorname{rank}A$. The minimal recurrence degree of any prefix, computed by discrepancy updates in the Berlekamp–Massey algorithm, is consequently a certified lower bound for the rational rank. Choice of probe can reduce a lower bound but cannot increase it beyond the true rank.

Let $g_L$ be the independently computed dimension of the rational quadratic-product restrictions at length $L$. Once $\operatorname{rank}Q_{L-1}$ is certified, $\dim C^L-\operatorname{rank}Q_{L-1}-g_L$ is an upper bound for $\operatorname{rank}Q_L$. The replay stops the sparse recurrence when the lower bound reaches this number, and advances to the next length only upon equality. An inconclusive probe returns its attained lower bound. The native loop uses sparse matrix–vector products without forming $M$. [Certificate driver](krylov.py) · [Finite-field kernel](krylov.cpp)

At $D=10$, in order of holomorphic totals $h=2,\ldots,10$, the separately balanced relative dimensions are $20,101,270,539,942,808,646,241,74$. Every rational lower bound is attained. Thus through $D=10$ all 179,665 relative classes, or 359,330 absolute classes counting the constant-ghost partners, survive every quantum differential. Of the relative classes, 179,180 have holomorphic derivative degree at least two. The counts include all finite field lengths and labeled source multidegrees in this range. A finite-rank singular-supported lift beyond this range is unresolved.
