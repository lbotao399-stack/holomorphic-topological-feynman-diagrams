# The scalar box and the first exceptional Hessian

Let $x,y$ be the two undifferentiated scalar colors. On a bihomogeneous polynomial $F(x,y)$, the bare one-loop box detects the antisymmetric part of its mixed Hessian for every complex simple gauge algebra. In particular its kernel in bidegree $(p,q)$ is exactly the $(p,q)$ polarization of the degree-$p+q$ polynomials on one color. Requiring constant-color invariance restricts that space to the invariant polynomials. Every polynomial in this kernel is a strict cocycle. ## Box test

The two scalar derivatives supply the two odd coordinates in the root Berezin integral. Thus every output field of the box is a first spatial derivative of the ghost. The ordered simplex has volume $1/6$. Assign to the three independent derivatives of the ghost three odd constants times the same ordinary color $Z$. The determinant of the three spatial directions and the Grassmann reordering have equal signs, so the six permutations cancel the denominator. The two assignments of $x,y$ to the root ends have opposite odd signs. With the action pairing identifying a mixed Hessian with an endomorphism, the resulting color contraction pairs its skew part with $(\operatorname{ad}_Z)^3$. The action and edge conventions multiply the test by the same nonzero box prefactor. Polarization in $Z$ recovers the general ghost coefficient. Each graph at loop order $n\ge2$ differentiates at least three scalars at one odd point, so it vanishes already on the bare $F$.

The cubes span the full skew endomorphism space $\Lambda^2\mathfrak g$. Their span is invariant under every automorphism of $\mathfrak g$. The exterior square of the adjoint is the direct sum of the adjoint and its bracket kernel. The latter is irreducible for each non-$A$ simple type. For $\mathfrak{sl}_N$, $N\ge3$, its two irreducible summands are exchanged by the outer diagram involution. For $\mathfrak{sl}_2$ the kernel is zero. These are multiplicity-free decompositions. [Adjoint square, Eq. 20](https://doi.org/10.1140/epjc/s10052-025-14943-y) · [Type-$A$ constituents, Eq. 10](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.physletb.2025.139695)

The trace pairing of $(\operatorname{ad}_H)^3$ with $\operatorname{ad}_H$ is the sum of the fourth powers of the real root values for $H$ in the real Cartan, positive for nonzero $H$. This detects the adjoint summand. At rank at least two, choose adjacent simple roots $\alpha,\beta$ and a regular $H$ for which $\alpha(H),\beta(H)>0$. If $(\operatorname{ad}_H)^3=\operatorname{ad}_K$, commutation with the whole Cartan forces $K$ to be in the Cartan. Evaluating on the three roots $\alpha,\beta,\alpha+\beta$ would give
$$
0=(\alpha(H)+\beta(H))^3-\alpha(H)^3-\beta(H)^3
=3\alpha(H)\beta(H)(\alpha(H)+\beta(H))>0 .
$$
This detects the bracket kernel, and the outer automorphism detects both summands whenever it splits. Thus vanishing of the box forces the mixed Hessian to be symmetric. Each bidegree coefficient is already symmetric inside its $p$ slots of $x$ and its $q$ slots of $y$. Mixed Hessian symmetry supplies the transpositions between the two groups. Their full symmetry is exactly polarization of the corresponding single-color polynomial. If either bidegree is zero the result holds directly.

## The eighth $F_4$ class

In an orthonormal Cartan frame the short roots are the signed coordinate units and the 16 half-sign vectors. Summing $(2\alpha(x))^6$ over all short roots $\alpha$ gives a sextic Weyl invariant $f$. Its Chevalley extension to $\mathfrak f_4$ is unique. Take the trace of the product of its two Hessian endomorphisms,
$$
F(x,y)=\operatorname{Tr}_{\mathfrak f_4}\big(\nabla^2 f(x)\nabla^2 f(y)\big).
$$
At a regular Cartan $x$, differentiating equivariance of $\nabla f$ in an orbit direction makes the Hessian act on the $\alpha$ root space by $\alpha(\nabla f(x))/\alpha(x)$. Its Cartan block is the ordinary Cartan Hessian. Thus the restriction of $F$ is the Cartan Hessian contraction plus
$$
\sum_{\substack{\alpha\in\Phi\\\mathrm{roots}}}
\frac{\alpha(\nabla f(x))\alpha(\nabla f(y))}{\alpha(x)\alpha(y)} .
$$
Both expressions have polynomial extensions to all pairs. The full color contraction supplies an explicit gauge-invariant local insertion.

The 1,152 orthogonal Cartan matrices consist of 384 signed permutations and 768 half-sign Hadamard matrices. Taking their two symmetric-power characters gives dimension eight in scalar bidegree $(4,4)$ and dimension three in degree eight on a single Cartan. The seven products of polarized basic invariants in bidegree $(4,4)$ are independent. At 23 fixed regular integer pairs their exact evaluation rank rises from seven to eight after adjoining either the Cartan Hessian contraction or the full adjoint expression above. The same character computation and polarizations agree in all other bidegrees with both entries at most four. Independently, the highest-root wedge with the root one simple step below it has highest weight $(2,1,1,0)$ in this Cartan frame. Its Weyl dimension is $1274=\binom{52}{2}-52$, verifying the exterior-square decomposition in the case used here. ## One-loop boundary in the commuting triple

The three odd ghost jets in the spatial determinant have symmetric color coefficients. Polarizing one ordinary third color $Z$ therefore gives precisely their graded cochain. This replaces the respective commutators $[x,Z],[y,Z]$ by the classical boundaries made with scalar jets, and $[x,y]$ by the boundary made with $b$. All other first-mode letters are closed in the relative complex. Thus every invariant in the ideal generated by these three brackets gives a classical boundary after polarization. An invariant primitive follows by averaging, or by using equivariant word reorderings throughout. [Maximal-length boundary calculation](relative.md)

The entire space of degree $(3,3,3)$ invariant polynomials in $x,y,Z$ has dimension 107. For an independent count, form the character of $S^3\mathfrak f_4$ from the 48 roots and four zero weights. The Weyl character numerator gives, each with multiplicity one, the doubled orthonormal highest weights
$$
(3,1,1,1),\quad(2,2,0,0),\quad(4,2,2,0),\quad(6,2,0,0),\quad(6,6,0,0).
$$
For every ordered pair $\lambda,\nu$ of these weights sum the signed $S^3\mathfrak f_4$ weight multiplicity at $w(\nu+\rho)-(\lambda+\rho)$ over the 1,152 Weyl elements. Here $\rho$ is the half sum of positive roots in the same doubled coordinates. Summing these tensor-product multiplicities gives 107. [Exact character computation](f4.py)

Realize the 52 colors as derivations of the 27-dimensional algebra of Hermitian three-by-three octonionic matrices. The Jordan product and its trace metric give a totally symmetric invariant cubic tensor. Products of word traces on this representation have evaluation rank at least 103. In the following four contractions place one copy of the cubic at each of two vertices and insert the three displayed matrix words on their three connecting lines. The digits $0,1,2$ stand for $x,y,Z$, respectively, in their written order:
$$
(01,100,2122),\quad(1,202,12010),\quad(2,0210,1021),\quad(00,12,12021).
$$
Together with the trace products they have rank 107 on 107 samples in the prime field of order 1009. The samples are reductions of integer linear combinations of commutators of Jordan left multiplications. The replay verifies all 52 selected matrices preserve the cubic product and the metric over the integers. The only denominators in the sample contractions are powers of two from the inverse metric. The nonzero minor is therefore a rational lower bound, and the Weyl character gives its exact upper bound. [Jordan realization, §4.2](https://doi.org/10.1090/S0273-0979-01-00934-X) · [Exact bracket-ideal certificate](f4_box_boundary.py)

Every color matrix is skew relative to the trace metric. Modulo the three brackets, transposing a word of length $l$ reverses its letters, which can be reordered at the cost of the sign $(-1)^l$. A trace product of total degree nine contains an odd trace and vanishes there. Transpose all three words of a cubic contraction and interchange its identical endpoint tensors. This returns the same scalar times $(-1)^9$ modulo the same brackets. Thus all 107 polynomials are in the bracket ideal. More generally, in total degree nine every polynomial $GL_3$ representation in the commuting-triple quotient would contain its balanced weight $(3,3,3)$. Hence the full degree-nine quotient of invariants on three commuting $F_4$ colors is zero, with the commutator scheme structure retained. The box of every degree-eight scalar is consequently classically exact. The highest relative cohomology quotient at the Hessian's one-loop target vanishes in the spatial determinant component.
