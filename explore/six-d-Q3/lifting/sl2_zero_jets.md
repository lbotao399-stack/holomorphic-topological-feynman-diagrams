# Complete zero-holomorphic-jet sector for $\mathfrak{sl}_2$

At source weights $(p,q)$, which count $\theta_1,\theta_2$ on the input fields, the constant-color relative cohomology contains one scalar class when $p+q$ is even. It contains one class with a single $b$ when $p,q\ge1$ and $p+q$ is odd. All other relative spaces vanish. Absolute cohomology has one further copy of each class multiplied classically by $\operatorname{tr}(c^3)$. Every displayed class survives all quantum spectral differentials. The leading representative uses zero holomorphic derivatives. Its quantum completion can contain holomorphic derivatives.

## The three minor equations

Write $x=g_1$, $y=g_2$ and let $S$ be the polynomial algebra in their six color components. Set the constant ghost $c$ to zero in the relative differential. Then $\widehat Q_0x=\widehat Q_0y=0$, $\widehat Q_0b=[x,y]$. The bracket identifies $\Lambda^2\mathfrak{sl}_2$ with $\mathfrak{sl}_2$. Thus the three components of $[x,y]$ are the three minors of the two-column matrix $(x,y)$ up to an invertible constant change of generators. In the Koszul complex on these minors write $f=x\times y$ for their ordered triple, using color coordinates in an orthogonal frame for the action pairing. Any common nonconstant divisor of all three minors is excluded: for instance $x_2y_3-x_3y_2$ is irreducible and does not divide $x_3y_1-x_1y_3$. Hence their greatest common divisor is one.

If $h\cdot f=0$ with $h\in S^3$, then $h=A x+B y$ over the fraction field, since $x,y$ span the plane orthogonal to $f$. The component equations
$$
h\times y=A f,\qquad x\times h=B f
$$
force the reduced denominators of $A,B$ to divide all three minors. Hence $A,B\in S$, uniquely. The degree-one Koszul cycles thus have generators
$$
U=\operatorname{tr}(xb),\qquad V=\operatorname{tr}(yb).
$$
Boundaries have coefficient vector $w\times f=x(w\cdot y)-y(w\cdot x)$ with arbitrary polynomial vector $w$. In degree two the cycle condition is $h\times f=0$, so $h=Df$ over the fraction field, and again $D\in S$. These are precisely the degree-three boundaries. The degree-three cycle condition is $Df=0$, forcing $D=0$. Degree zero gives $S/(f)$. Factors from the invertible color change cancel in these kernels and images.

## Color invariants and absolute ghosts

Set $A=\operatorname{tr}(x^2)$, $B=\operatorname{tr}(xy)$, $D=\operatorname{tr}(y^2)$. The invariant scalar polynomials are $\mathbb C[A,B,D]$. The equivariant polynomial color vectors are generated over this algebra by $x,y,[x,y]$. For a direct trace reduction, use $\mathfrak{sl}_2=\mathrm{Sym}^2(\mathbb C^2)$ as a group representation. Invariant contractions of fundamental slots are spanned by pairings with the two-dimensional alternating form: tensoring a symmetric power with one fundamental splits into the fully symmetrized tensor and the alternating contraction to the preceding symmetric power. Iterating gives these pairings. Their cycles on traceless matrix slots give products of traces. The identity $xy+yx=B\mathbf 1$, together with $2x^2=A\mathbf 1$ and $2y^2=D\mathbf 1$, reduces a word to the span of $1,x,y,xy$. Pairing a word with one extra traceless matrix gives exactly $x,y,[x,y]$ as covariants. The Gram entries of a generic pair vary independently.

Taking color invariants is exact at each finite bidegree. In degree zero the invariant ideal is generated by contracting $f$ with the three covariants. Only $[x,y]$ gives a nonzero contraction, with $\operatorname{tr}([x,y]^2)=2(B^2-AD)$. In degree one the three choices of covariant $w$ in the boundary formula give the relations
$$
BU-AV=0,\qquad DU-BV=0 .
$$
The third covariant gives zero. Thus the scalar ring and its degree-one module are detected by diagonal evaluation. On $x=tH,y=sH,b=\varepsilon H$ with $H=\operatorname{diag}(1,-1)$ and odd $\varepsilon$, the five generators restrict to
$$
(A,B,D,U,V)=(2t^2,2ts,2s^2,2t\varepsilon,2s\varepsilon).
$$
The scalar ring is the even polynomials in $t,s$. The $b$ module is the odd polynomials times $\varepsilon$. Indeed $a(t,s)t+b(t,s)s=0$ with $a,b$ even implies $(a,b)=(s\gamma,-t\gamma)$ with $\gamma$ odd. Writing $\gamma=t\gamma_1+s\gamma_2$ with even coefficients gives exactly the two displayed relations after the common trace factors are restored. This proves the bidegree count and identifies the surviving $b$ on diagonal pairs as the odd Cartan direction.

Restoring $c$ gives the ordinary constant-color Lie algebra cochains with coefficients in this Koszul complex. At each finite weight a color-equivariant splitting into homology and contractible pairs exists by complete reducibility. For $\mathfrak{sl}_2$ the constant-color cohomology with finite-dimensional coefficients $M$ has $M^{\mathfrak{sl}_2}$ in degrees zero and three and vanishes in degrees one and two. This follows from the Whitehead lemmas and exterior duality in dimension three. The additional factor is $\operatorname{tr}(c^3)$, the invariant alternating volume on three constant colors. Counting field length by $l$ and odd coordinate weights by $u,v$, the absolute series becomes
$$
(1+l^3)\frac{1+l^2uv+l^2uv(u+v)}{(1-l^2u^2)(1-l^2v^2)} .
$$

## Quantum differential

The three scalar generators and $U,V$ belong to the strict quadratic sector. The constant-ghost cubic is also strict: $\operatorname{tr}(c^4)=0$ by graded cyclicity and two ghost derivatives already multiply the square of $\theta_1\theta_2$. Marked multiplication therefore gives finite lifts of all the counted classes. At loop order $m$ the homogeneous holomorphic derivative degree increases by $3m$ from the current determinants; displacement insertions transfer each original derivative to one output. Consequently $d_r$ increases the holomorphic degree of the leading representative by $3r$. No class with zero holomorphic derivatives can receive an incoming differential.
