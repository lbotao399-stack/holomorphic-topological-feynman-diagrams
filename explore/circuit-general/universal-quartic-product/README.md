# Universal cancellation in a complete higher-superpotential product

The full three-loop product with inputs

\[
\beta^{1i_1}(\partial_{+\dot a}\beta^{2i_2})\gamma_5,
\qquad b_A\beta^{3i_3}
\]

has a rational answer for an arbitrary pure-matter superpotential tensor
and arbitrary representations. All input and output color indices, all
surviving-field sources, and the free spinor index remain open.
The field \(\gamma_5\) is free, neutral, and absent from the
superpotential; it can be set to one.

The complete quartic calculation has **28 Wick matchings, 12 original
color words, and 10 rational color words** after the representation
relation. The entire \(\zeta(3)\) contribution is the sum of the two
explicitly computed full spinors multiplying the exact tensor defects

\[
\bigl([t_A,t_B]-iC^C{}_{AB}t_C\bigr)^{i_1}{}_{j_1}
\bar W^{j_1i_2i_3i_4},
\qquad
\bigl([t_A,t_B]-iC^C{}_{AB}t_C\bigr)^{i_2}{}_{j_2}
\bar W^{i_1j_2i_3i_4}.
\]

Both vanish by the representation relation. The calculation does not need
gauge invariance of \(\bar W\), and therefore includes every valid
gauge-invariant choice. It does not infer cancellation from an individual
scalar kernel or from a selected color entry.

## Arbitrary degree and all-order termination for these inputs

For a polarized homogeneous pure \(\bar W(\gamma)\) of degree at least
three, the entire matching census per fixed ordered choice of the three
input slots is

\[
16\quad\text{commutator matchings},\qquad
8\quad\text{source-free cubic contractions to SP},\qquad
4(\deg\bar W-3)\quad\text{generic SP matchings}.
\]

Polarization slots are computational tensor slots, not additional physical
species. The separate physical \(\gamma_5\) remains free. The exact
factorial normalization and all differentiated-slot assignments recover
the original polynomial, including repeated physical fields.

If \(n\) is the interaction order and \(r\) counts pure-superpotential
vertices, the four input antifields imply

\[
n+r\leq4.
\]

With no superpotential vertex, the available contractible even fields give
\(2n\leq n+1\). Thus every order at least four vanishes. Orders at most
two have only SP geometric graphs; the complete third-order coefficient
is rational by the tensor identity above.

This proves **all-order rationality and termination for this fixed
first-derivative input family**, over the rational ring generated by the
original interaction coefficients and superpotential tensor, with the
universal \((8\pi^2)^{-n}\) factors restored. It does not assert numerical
rationality of arbitrary coupling values, evaluate every lower-order
formula, or establish the same claim for arbitrary inputs,
\(\bar W(\gamma,w)\), derivative superpotentials, higher input jets,
or quantum-closed observable classes.
