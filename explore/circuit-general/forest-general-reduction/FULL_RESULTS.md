# All sourced-ear example values

These are all seven examples and all twenty-one exact values in [ear_certificate.json](ear_certificate.json). Each example includes its bare kernel and the entire first-current spinor. The source assignment, ordered original graph, marked vertices, current edge, reduction steps and every intermediate size bound are recorded in that certificate.

The free spinor index is $+\dot a$. The two source spinors $\lambda_0,\lambda_1$ belong to the original vertices zero and one. They are linearly independent in every listed source assignment. The current results below use those original spinors as a basis and retain both components. These are the exact values at the recorded source assignments, not formulas asserting arbitrary-source dependence with the same constant coefficients.

The higher-superpotential source patterns are individual geometric kernels. Full operator Wick sums require the additional coupling and representation coefficients, as in [the complete quartic calculations](../nonrenormalizable-product/README.md).
