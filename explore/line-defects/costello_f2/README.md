# Quadratic coupling coefficient in the formal PBW center

Work at the fixed nonzero Moyal parameter of Costello §16, in the stable flavor family. Write $\kappa$ for the lift of the Lie algebra identity, the marked trace of §11. The relation with the unmarked disk is in the [parent README](../README.md). At order two and after fixing the vacuum coupling, the unknown base change has the form
$$
\hbar_{\rm QFT}=32\pi^2\hbar_{\rm comb}+b\kappa\hbar_{\rm comb}^2
\pmod{\hbar_{\rm comb}^3}.
$$

**Cohomology projection.** The first-order class has two PBW outputs. Multiplication by $\kappa$ gives three outputs. In the stable invariant complex, project the two-trace part onto traces with respectively one and two outputs. On the first factor keep only zero input slots and evaluate the coefficient of the Weyl unit in the Weyl-symbol basis. This is a chain map to the single-trace complex. The classical Chevalley–Eilenberg differential increases input arity and preserves trace and output counts. The stable cyclic decomposition realizes it as the sum of the differentials on the individual trace factors. The projection takes $\kappa[\mu_1]$ to the first-order class $[\mu_1]$. Its nonvanishing and the identification of relative tangent cohomology with absolute $H^2$ are inputs from Costello §§16.5–16.7 and Appendix A. A single choice of matrix entries is sufficient to check a color tensor; it does not by itself detect cohomology. [Independent four-flavor test with value $-3$ on the first-order generator](projection_check.md)

**Principal symbol.** Give every generator PBW degree one. At first order the bracket has at most two outputs. At second order its three-output component is the principal symbol. Formally substitute $\hbar_{\rm comb}=s\eta$ and multiply each generator by $s$ before taking the coefficient of $s$ in their commutator. Equivalently, use the associated graded of the PBW commutator. A first-order redefinition with two outputs changes the second-order principal symbol through insertions with one output-to-input contraction. This follows from the Leibniz rule in the commutative associated graded. An insertion between two single-trace symbols joins their flavor cycles. First-order principal symbols agree in cohomology; the differential preserves trace number and PBW degree, so their connecting one-cochain may be projected to the single-trace, two-output part. Changes of degree at most one cannot contribute at three outputs. Second-order changes of generators contribute exact cochains.

**ADHM principal symbol.** The scaled open generator for the matrix word $u$ is $\eta^{-1}IuJ$ in the ordinary commutative oscillator algebra at $s=0$ (matrices still multiply in order). The commutator divided by $s$ is its classical ADHM Poisson bracket. Contracting a framing pair between two open words joins their ends. Contracting an adjoint pair reconnects the two open words across the chosen letters. Both are single cyclic flavor tensors in the two inputs and the outputs, by the trace-dual matrix-unit contraction. Reordering letters in any resulting open word with the classical moment relation replaces $XY-YX$ by $c\,\mathrm{Id}-IJ$ in gauge indices. The $IJ$ term splits the open word into two open words joined at one summed flavor label. Neither operation produces an isolated flavor trace. In particular the singleton projection vanishes for every pair of words. The exact low-mode presentation (A3), with $n=1$, independently contains at most quadratic output terms. [GRZ, §§2.0, 2.3](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00220-025-05488-y)

**QFT bare graphs.** The three-output relation in the Koszul dual comes from the ternary operation on three linear ghost insertions with two surviving fields (Gaiotto–Oh (B.7)). For a connected Wick graph with $n$ such insertions, $V$ bulk action vertices and $E$ propagators, its coupling power is $E-V=n-1+b_1$ before quantum counterterms. At $n=3$ and order two it is a tree. Contracting trace-dual adjoint indices along each tree edge joins two trace cycles. With five external slots the result has one flavor cycle. An order-$j$ quantum counterterm adds $j$ to the count.

For a disconnected forest with projected trace sizes $4+1$, the component carrying the isolated marked output has just one leg, unless both traces belong to a connected component and the rest is vacuum. Vacuum factors cancel in normalized correlators. With two components and three marked inputs there is just one loop available at order two. A trivalent one-loop graph with one leg has one vertex and one self-contraction. With an undescended ghost its vacuum expectation also vanishes by ghost number. For the descended insertion its tadpole has holomorphic momenta $0,k,-k$ at the vertex. The Moyal phase is one and the color commutator contracted with the graded-symmetric inverse trace pairing is zero. A linear bulk counterterm of order one would replace this tadpole; the tadpole vanishes in heat gauge and the two independent bulk quantization ambiguities in §7 contain no linear interaction. [Costello, §§7–9,16](https://arxiv.org/abs/1705.02500) · [Four-plus-one color test](../costello_two_trace.py)

**Transfer on a genuine polydisc.** The ternary operation uses a compact path between binary compositions of disjoint intervals. At finite separation the graph integrals and scalar subtractions preserve their color tensors. On a smooth Dolbeault form of simultaneous rotation weight $k$, let $R_r(z)=rz$ for $0<r\le 1$, and let $B$ generate antiholomorphic radial dilation. The contraction and its projection are
$$
H\omega=2\int_0^1r^{-k-1}R_r^*\iota_B\omega\,dr,
\qquad
P\omega=\lim_{r\downarrow0}r^{-k}R_r^*\omega.
$$
For a Taylor monomial with antiholomorphic multi-index $\beta$ and form degree $q$, its radial exponent is $k+2(|\beta|+q)$. The projection retains the holomorphic homogeneous Taylor part; the integral on each nonzero contraction has denominator $|\beta|+q$. Taylor remainder bounds near the origin give convergence on smooth weight-homogeneous forms. Cartan’s formula and twice the antiholomorphic Euler operator being the radial Euler operator minus $k$ give $\bar\partial H+H\bar\partial=1-P$. A holomorphic derivative lowers $k$ by one and commutes with $\iota_B$, with $\partial_zR_r^*=rR_r^*\partial_z$. Hence $[\partial_z,H]=0$. The interval has its ordinary integration homotopy. On several slots the radial pullback is a product and $\iota_B$ is a sum of insertions on a single slot. These linear maps preserve separate color tensors and commute with separate circle rotations on disconnected components, also when they share a radial integration variable. Transposition gives the contraction on distributional polynomial observables. [Polynomial check](transfer.py) · [Gaiotto–Oh, Appendices A–B](https://doi.org/10.1007/JHEP12(2024)184)

Perturbing the free contraction by the classical BRST bracket splits a leaf at a single-trace vertex. A Wick or BV contraction joining two slots costs one power of $\hbar$. The quantum transfer therefore obeys the preceding forest count at order two. As an independent test, the isolated undifferentiated marked ghost, its time descendant, the scalar vacuum, the polydisc radii and the interval path all have circle weight zero. A bare one-loop contribution carries $\hbar c^j$ with $j\ge0$, of weight $2(1+j)$. Its scalar projection vanishes. A bulk one-loop finite counterterm could replace the loop in a one-point tree only with valence one; the two bulk ambiguities in §7 have valence at least two. An exact second-order change of the comparison on local operators maps to an exact cochain under the singleton projection.

With this transfer, both principal symbols have zero singleton projection. Its cohomology class is $b[\mu_1]$, giving $b=0$ under the nonzero first-order class and the degree bound of Costello §16. The answer concerns the formal center lifting the Lie algebra identity at fixed nonzero $c$. The unmarked charge disk and fixed-rank specialization have the distinct filtration behavior computed above.
